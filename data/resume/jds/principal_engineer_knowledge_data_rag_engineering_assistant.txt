Who we are looking for We are looking for an experienced Architect to lead enterprise architecture for knowledge and data engineering, RAG, and Graph RAG. Experience in Agentic AI, orchestration, testing, evaluation, and governance is desirable. Working with architecture, data, AI, security, risk, product, testing, and application teams, you will turn business strategy into practical architectures, standards, roadmaps, and delivery controls. You will bring strong architectural leadership and expertise in semantic technologies, modern data platforms, and retrieval systems. Why this role is important to us Enterprise AI depends on trusted, well-engineered, permission-aware, traceable, and continuously validated data and knowledge. This role establishes the architectural foundation that connects source systems, data pipelines, semantic models, knowledge graphs, retrieval services, models, agents, controls, testing, and business workflows. The Architect will reduce fragmented point solutions, promote reusable capabilities, guide investment decisions, and ensure that AI applications and agents retrieve reliable evidence, generate grounded responses, execute approved actions, and remain secure, observable, auditable, resilient, and compliant throughout their lifecycle. About the team — Alpha Intelligence Alpha Intelligence is State Street’s AI & Automation Center of Excellence for Alpha Implementations — the team rewiring how the industry’s first front-to-back platform gets delivered. We turn the deep expertise locked inside our implementation teams into engineered processes, governed knowledge and intelligent agents that cut manual effort, compress client go-live timelines and make every implementation faster and more predictable than the last. We operate as one global team across the North America, EMEA, and APAC, combining process engineering, automation delivery, AI platform engineering and responsible-AI governance under a single roof. We work on real client implementations, not pilots that sit on a shelf — what we build is adopted, measured and scaled. If you want to shape how AI is applied to one of the most complex delivery landscapes in financial services, this is the team to do it in. What you will be responsible for Own the enterprise architecture for Knowledge Engineering, Data Engineering, RAG and Graph RAG, Agentic AI, testing, and governance, aligning strategy, platforms, delivery, controls, and measurable outcomes. Define the enterprise vision, target architecture, standards, reusable patterns, and roadmap across data, knowledge, retrieval, AI agents, testing, and governance. Translate business priorities into scalable solution architectures, transition plans, investment decisions, and measurable outcomes. Lead knowledge and data architecture, including ontologies, semantic models, knowledge graphs, metadata, lineage, data pipelines, quality, and lifecycle management. Architect production-grade RAG and Graph RAG services covering ingestion, indexing, hybrid retrieval, ranking, grounding, citations, provenance, and feedback. Establish testing, evaluation, and release standards for data quality, retrieval, prompts. Embed testing, monitoring, traceability, rollback, and governance controls in delivery pipelines. Lead architecture reviews, align stakeholders, and guide technical teams. What we value The successful candidate will combine strategic thinking, enterprise architecture perspective, deep technical credibility, and a quality-and-risk mindset. The candidate must communicate complex architectural choices, test evidence, control requirements, risks, and trade-offs clearly to engineering teams, business stakeholders, governance forums, auditors, and senior technology leaders. We value pragmatic architecture, business-value alignment, secure-by-design and test-by-design practices, reuse, interoperability, measurable quality, transparent decision making, and continuously governed enterprise AI outcomes. These skills will help you succeed in this role: Must-have skills Strong experience in enterprise architecture, technical leadership, and working with cross-functional teams. Strong data engineering expertise across ETL/ELT, batch and streaming pipelines, lakehouse or warehouse platforms, data quality, and DataOps. Expertise in designing ontologies and semantic models and implementing end-to-end RAG and Graph RAG pipelines, including ingestion, chunking, embeddings, indexing, hybrid retrieval, semantic search, graph traversal, ranking, grounding, provenance, and evaluation. Strong knowledge of Responsible AI, privacy, access control, model risk, auditability, regulatory compliance, and policy enforcement. Experience delivering solutions in financial services or another regulated industry. Nice-to-have skills Exposure to modern data, streaming, workflow, monitoring, vector database, and enterprise search tools such as Snowflake, Databricks, Kafka, dbt, or Airflow would be useful. Familiarity with AI platforms or frameworks such as Azure AI Foundry, Copilot Studio, Vertex AI, LangChain, LangGraph, LlamaIndex, Semantic Kernel, or MLflow would be beneficial. Awareness of Model Context Protocol, connecting AI agents with tools, access controls, monitoring, and AI security would be helpful. Experience establishing automated testing, quality gates, monitoring, traceability, release, and rollback controls within CI/CD and MLOps/LLMOps. An understanding of cloud costs, capacity planning, and communicating architecture proposals to senior stakeholders would be useful. Relevant certifications in architecture, cloud, data, graph, testing, security, governance, or Generative AI would be a plus. Education & Preferred Qualifications A bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Artificial Intelligence, Data Engineering, Information Systems, or a related field is preferred; a master’s degree would be a plus. Extensive technology experience, ideally including architecture, data or knowledge engineering, software or AI engineering, testing, governance, or enterprise platforms. Experience providing architectural guidance and contributing to standards, roadmaps, governance, and key design decisions would be valuable. Exposure to delivering secure, scalable, and well-governed data platforms, knowledge graphs, ontologies, RAG or Graph RAG services, Agentic AI, or Generative AI solutions would be beneficial. The ability to work with senior stakeholders and bring business, architecture, engineering, testing, cloud, security, risk, and operations teams together would be helpful. Relevant certifications in architecture, cloud, data, graph, testing, security, governance, or Generative AI would be a plus.
