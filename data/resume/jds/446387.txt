You will influence architecture across multiple components and teams while remaining technically hands-on. Design HLD/LLD, service boundaries, APIs, data flows, and integration patterns.  Drive architectural modernization of large C++ codebases.  Define technical approaches for migrating legacy applications to cloud-native SaaS architectures.  Evaluate architectural trade-offs involving performance, scalability, reliability, maintainability, and cost.  Identify and drive resolution of architectural and technical debt.  Work closely with architects and technical leaders across teams.       C++ and Performance:     Remain hands-on in modern C++17/C++20 development.  Design high-performance algorithms and concurrent systems.  Lead performance investigations involving: CPU, Memory, Network, I/O, Concurrency, Serialization, Database access.  Establish performance benchmarks and measurable engineering improvements.  Review complex C++ designs and implementations.       Distributed Systems and Cloud:     Design highly scalable distributed services.  Define approaches for horizontal scaling, high availability, fault tolerance, Self-healing, stateful connections, distributed caching, and event-driven processing. Drive production readiness and operational excellence.       Production and Reliability:     Lead investigation of critical production issues.  Drive root-cause analysis and long-term corrective actions.  Improve observability through metrics, logging, and distributed tracing.  Define reliability and scalability improvements based on production telemetry.  Champion automation to minimize manual operational intervention.
